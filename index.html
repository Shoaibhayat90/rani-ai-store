Fair questions.

How do payment plans work?

Choose "50% deposit" on any package to start with half down. The balance is due on delivery, and full ownership of everything — design, files, content — transfers to you once the balance is paid.

What if I need changes after delivery?

Every package includes 2 revision rounds. If you need more, extra rounds are $150 each, quoted up front — no surprise invoices.

How fast is delivery?

Launch Website: 7 days from kickoff. Shopify Store Build: 14 days. POS Setup, AI Chatbot, and SEO Foundation: within 10 days. The clock starts once we've had the kickoff call and received your content.

Do I own everything?

Yes. On full payment, the design, the files, and the content are 100% yours — domain, hosting accounts, and all.

What if you miss the deadline?

If we're 30 or more days past the agreed delivery date and we've received everything we asked for from you, you get a full refund. We put that in writing before we start.
